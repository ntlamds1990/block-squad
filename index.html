Prototype · Meadow village

Block Squad

Goblins have raided the meadow village. Lead your squad of four heroes and drive them out.

  • Tap a hero, then a blue tile to move. Each hero gets 2 actions a turn.
  • Tap a goblin on a red tile to see your hit chance, then tap again to attack. Attacking ends that hero's turn.
  • Walls are cover. Low walls make you harder to hit, tall walls even more. A miss still grazes for 1.
  • Ember's Fireball blows up blocks. Digby builds walls and digs them out.
  • Drag to turn the camera, pinch to zoom.
